HEVC vs H.264 on iPhone: which one to choose

One makes smaller files, the other plays almost everywhere.

Checked 23 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Zipvid

Short answerHEVC (H.265) usually makes files noticeably smaller than H.264 at similar quality, but H.264 is the safer choice for sharing and for older devices. Use High Efficiency for storage, and Most Compatible (or an H.264 export) when you share outside the Apple world. Savings vary with the video.

Steps

  1. Open Settings → Camera → Formats.
  2. Choose High Efficiency for smaller files (HEVC).
  3. Choose Most Compatible for H.264 if you share often with older devices or non-Apple platforms.
  4. For an existing video, export or compress a copy in the format you need.

Apple notes that media can be converted to H.264 when you import it to a PC or share it with a device that does not support HEVC.

FAQ

Is HEVC the same as H.265?

Yes. HEVC (High Efficiency Video Coding) is the same codec as H.265.

How much smaller is HEVC?

Sources say about half the size in the best case and around 20 to 25% in one test. Savings depend on the video, so do not expect a fixed number.
