Why videos take so much space on iPhone

What decides the size of a video, and the settings that make future videos smaller.

Checked 23 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Zipvid

Short answerVideo size comes from resolution, frame rate, HDR and format. Apple suggests choosing a resolution below the default 1080p, turning off HDR Video, and making sure High Efficiency is selected to keep future files smaller. For videos you already have, compress a copy and delete the original.

Steps

  1. Settings → Camera → Record Video: choose a lower resolution than you need.
  2. Turn off HDR Video if you do not need it.
  3. Settings → Camera → Formats: choose High Efficiency.
  4. Compress big videos you already have, then delete the originals and empty Recently Deleted.

One guide also notes that 30 fps instead of 60 fps can cut file size noticeably with a small visible difference. Treat that as a rule of thumb.

FAQ

Why is a one-minute video so large?

At the default 1080p, Apple estimates roughly 70 to 105 MB per minute for iMovie exports; 4K and 60 fps are larger.

Will deleting the original free space immediately?

Deleted items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Empty that album to get the space back right away.
