How to send a large video by email from iPhone

Most email services cap attachments near 25 MB. Here are the ways around it.

Checked 23 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Zipvid

Short answerGmail, Yahoo Mail and ProtonMail allow about 25 MB, while Outlook and iCloud Mail allow about 20 MB. To send more, use Mail Drop (a link instead of an attachment), a cloud link, or AirDrop. Or compress the video under the limit and attach it.

Steps

  1. Check the video size and the limit of the recipient's mail service.
  2. Small overshoot: compress a copy to under 20 MB (safe for most providers) and attach it.
  3. Large video: use Mail Drop, or upload to Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive and paste the link.
  4. Nearby Apple device: AirDrop has no fixed size limit, but very large files can fail or be slow.

FAQ

Why does my email say the attachment is too large?

Most providers limit attachments to around 20 to 25 MB, and encoding makes the real message a bit bigger than the file.

Does compressing a video before emailing hurt quality?

It can. Aim for the smallest reduction that fits, and compare the result with the original.
