How to compress a video for Discord on iPhone

The Discord limit depends on your plan. Compress to about 10 MB and it works almost everywhere.

Checked 23 Nov 2026 · by Emil Svetlichnyy, developer of Zipvid

Short answerDiscord's file limit depends on the sender's plan, and guides disagree on the exact numbers (they quote 8 MB or 10 MB for free accounts and more for Nitro). A 10 MB target is the safe choice for most servers; check Discord's own help for the current limit.

Steps

  1. Check your plan and the current Discord limit.
  2. Compress a copy of the video to a target a little under the limit.
  3. Trim the clip to the part that matters. Short clips keep better quality at a small size.
  4. Upload the smaller copy in Discord and check that it plays.

FAQ

What is the Discord video size limit?

It depends on the plan and has changed over time. Guides quote 8 MB or 10 MB for free accounts and higher limits with Nitro, so verify it in Discord before you rely on a number.

How do I get a video under 10 MB?

Shorten it, lower the resolution, and compress to a 10 MB target.
